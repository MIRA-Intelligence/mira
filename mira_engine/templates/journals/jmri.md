# Journal of Magnetic Resonance Imaging — Research Article

## Sources and verification

- Official author guide: [JMRI Author Guidelines](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/15222586/homepage/forauthors.html)
- Official journal: [Journal of Magnetic Resonance Imaging](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/15222586)
- Checked: 2026-10-12. Basis: retrieved official guide, including manuscript
  organization, clinical reporting, graphics, AI and reference sections.
- Scope: initial Research Article. This profile summarizes selected requirements
  and adds separately labelled workflow instructions; it is not the entire guide.
  Use with [MIRA's shared delivery contract](common.md).

## Official requirements

### Limits, abstract and body

- ≤4,000 words; ≤50 references; ≤10 figures/tables combined.
- Abstract ≤300 words: Background; Purpose/Hypothesis; Study Type; Population
  (Subjects/Phantom/Specimen/Animal Model); Field Strength/Sequence; Assessment;
  Statistical Tests; Results; Data Conclusion.
- Follow with Evidence Level, Stage of Technical Efficacy and 3–6 keywords.
- Body: Introduction, Materials and Methods, Results, Discussion.
- Discussion ends with LIMITATIONS (penultimate paragraph), then CONCLUSION.

### Files, ethics and references

- Double-blind: identifying title/acknowledgments separate; figures separate;
  cover letter required; running title ≤40 characters.
- Begin Methods with ethics approval and consent/waiver.
- Number citations by appearance, in parentheses. List all authors through six;
  otherwise first three plus et al.
- TIFF/EPS artwork; minimum dpi: line art 1200, color 600, monochrome 300.

### AI, data and source discrepancies

- AI papers: code-access address; writing-AI acknowledgment includes tool/version,
  manufacturer and affected content.
- Most subjective interpretations require ≥3 independent observers.
- Results: exact p-values for nonsignificant findings; significant findings described
  as significant.
- Organization requires anonymity but Methods asks co-author observer initials.
- Text mandates Word; submission instructions also accommodate LaTeX.

## MIRA execution and clarification (not additional publisher rules)

### Intake and scientific evidence map

Confirm that the target is a clinical MRI research paper, not an automatically
inferred technical note or review. Use request_workflow_interaction when article
type, output expectations or the supplied evidence conflict. Keep the requested
editable DOCX draft even if another source format is later approved.

Construct a field-by-field abstract worksheet. For every field, attach the relevant
project evidence or mark it missing. Distinguish people, specimens, scans, lesions
and observations instead of treating their counts as interchangeable. Verify which
dataset served each modeling role, and which outcomes belong to a held-out test.

### Writing and reporting

Draft from the actual acquisition and analysis records. Build a protocol table in
working notes with scanner, sequence, relevant parameters, reader procedure,
reference measurement and comparison. Resolve ambiguous field strengths, sequence
names, units or endpoints before interpreting results. Do not fill absent protocol
values from typical scanner defaults.

For each finding, preserve the underlying effect, uncertainty and comparison in
an internal evidence ledger even if the journal's display style abbreviates the
statistical prose. Do not recompute unavailable statistics or omit inconvenient
findings to make the narrative cleaner. Frame a negative result according to the
evidence, not as proof of equivalence without an appropriate design.

Do not guess Evidence Level or Stage of Technical Efficacy from the title or
best metric. Ask the author to confirm the classification with the applicable
official definitions before finalizing it.

### File and declaration handling

Collect author identities, contribution claims, related-paper overlap, funding,
competing interests and real consent/approval information. Scan the reviewer copy,
figures and document metadata for identifying material. Where reader initials
would identify an author, flag the conflicting instructions and request resolution;
do not silently remove scientific information or declare an exception.

Use the supplied images to assemble publication figures. Check actual pixel sizes,
physical export dimensions, captions, units and legibility; changing a DPI tag alone
does not recover detail. Preserve original data and a record of transformations.
Do not manufacture a missing reader, consent statement, code repository or model
version.

Maintain ONE delivery candidate containing the draft and its associated files.
A suggested filename or checklist entry does not mean that file exists.

## Editorial recommendations (MIRA)

Make the MRI-based biological or diagnostic question explicit. Explain why each
sequence or measurement addresses it. Keep scanner-level technical performance
distinct from patient-level diagnostic evidence. Emphasize interpretability,
measurement reliability and realistic generalizability. Avoid claims about care
management, unseen populations or prospective performance without corresponding
evidence.

## Preflight and unresolved checks (MIRA)

Report counts, abstract-field coverage, export inspection, reference integrity and
declaration completeness. List author-owned unresolved items separately from
formatting defects. DOCX satisfies the current product and the guide's Word
instruction; do not label the same page's LaTeX pathway categorically forbidden.
The anonymity/observer-identification conflict requires clarification if triggered.
If an author requests a different statistical presentation, expose the difference
and obtain guidance rather than silently blending journals' styles. AI disclosure
must remain truthful even when exact tool metadata is unavailable. Consult the
current guide and decision letter for final portal or revision requirements.
