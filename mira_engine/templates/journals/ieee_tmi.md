## Official requirements

### Manuscript and disclosures — author guide

| Component | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Initial length | ≤10 pages including references |
| Layout | TMI template; two columns; single spacing |
| Submission manuscript | One PDF, <40 MB, with text/figures/tables/references |
| Abstract | One paragraph, ≤250 words; no abbreviations, citations, footnotes, equations or tables |
| Keywords | Index Terms after abstract |
| Author information | Affiliations/contact/funding in first-page footnote |
| References | IEEE numbering; all authors through six, otherwise first plus et al. |
| Biographies | Omit |
| Graphical abstract | Optional; peer-reviewed if supplied |

Conference extensions require the prior paper, citation and explanation of additions.
Previous rejection elsewhere requires disclosure and a changes/significance summary.
Ordinary cover letters are discouraged; prior review and conference extensions are
exceptions. AI-generated content needs an acknowledgment naming the system,
affected sections and use. Code/test-data sharing is recommended, not blanket
permission to publish private data. The submitting author needs ORCID.

### Administrative package — submission checklist

Use at least 10-point text. Include figures and tables in the paper, rather than
initially uploading them only as production artwork. The checklist prohibits
supplemental documents that extend the manuscript with extra prose, figures or
tables; allowed support includes graphical abstract, video/media, datasets, code
and the extended conference paper. Do not use a prose supplement to evade length.

Do not nominate reviewers in the cover letter. For prior rejection, supply the
decision letter and an executive summary addressing earlier concerns. Author emails
and affiliations must be accurate. Revisions/resubmissions are not subject to the
initial ten-page rule; consult their specific instructions.

### Stage distinctions — FAQ

Separate final-production figure files are requested at the final-file stage.
Accepted work can be cited as in press; under-review references allowed during
peer review must be resolved before final files. Equal first/corresponding
contributions may be stated in the first-page footnote. Submission-system contact
selection is distinct from that manuscript statement.

## MIRA execution and clarification (not additional publisher rules)

1. Identify the precise engineering contribution and the chosen experimental
   evidence. Confirm the submission stage and any previous journal decision before
   applying the initial-submission preset.
2. Treat the DOCX as the editable working deliverable and the rendered TMI PDF as
   a separately verified member of the SAME delivery candidate. An unrendered Word
   draft cannot establish a ten-page result.
3. Inspect the rendered document: actual page count, font size, column layout,
   equations, embedded figures, table overflow, file size and reading order.
   Record missing tools or template access instead of claiming formatting success.
4. If the paper exceeds the budget, use request_workflow_interaction to agree what
   to shorten or which claim to narrow. Do not hide essential methods or evaluations
   in a prohibited supplement and do not reduce font size to force a fit.
5. Obtain the real prior-version documents, author identities, grants, approval
   information, overlap explanations and AI-use details. Never manufacture a prior
   review history, ORCID, reviewer nomination or no-conflict assertion.
6. Separate local research evidence from material authorized for public sharing.
   A code-support file must contain an actual verified artifact; a proposed URL is
   not evidence of availability or reproducibility.

## Editorial recommendations (MIRA)

State the problem mathematically, distinguish assumptions from results and explain
the methodological advance. Build the argument from fair comparisons and supported
ablation, sensitivity, generalization or complexity evidence. Describe missing
evaluations as limitations, not implied completed experiments. Keep engineering
performance separate from unmeasured patient benefits. Preserve notation, units,
input-information boundaries and the exact comparator conditions.

## Preflight and unresolved checks (MIRA)

Prepare a compliance note with actual measurements and a manifest of available
submission files. Flag whether a document is for review, final production or local
working use. The guide discourages generic cover letters but also asks for a note
when including a graphical abstract: if that case applies, keep it limited to the
requested information and confirm current submission expectations. The checklist's
phrase about being “neither unpublished” conflicts with the guide's clear
unpublished-work requirement; do not interpret it as permission to submit already
published work without an eligible extension. Final fees, consent, licensing and
submission actions require author review; this profile authorizes none of them.
